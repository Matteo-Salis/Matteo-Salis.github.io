About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Météo-France, working on artificial intelligence for high-resolution medium-range weather forecasting as part of the WeatherGenerator project. My research interests lie at the intersection of machine learning, spatio-temporal data modelling, and environmental science, with a focus on data-driven approaches to understanding and predicting complex Earth-systems phenomena.

Previously, I worked on deep-learning methods for environmental applications, including groundwater resources, deforestation, and air-pollution analysis. My academic background includes studies in Modeling and Data Science, Environmental Economics, and Economics and Statistics.

Research interests

AI for weather forecasting; foundation models for weather and climate; physics-guided machine learning; environmental and Earth-system applications; explainable AI for spatio-temporal models.
